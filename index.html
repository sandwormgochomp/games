Thirty-Six Games

single-file HTML · no build step · keyboard + mouse · open one and play

Games

Aegis
Wave defense

Hold the ring against a data-driven bestiary. Earn refits between waves and rebuild the loadout that finally holds.

  • 100 levels
  • Canvas 2D
  • Bestiary
Beetlebound
Endless racer

Tap to jump, hold to fly, and outrun the rain that is always closing behind you. Flying is faster than running — the rain knows it too.

  • Jump + fly
  • Rain chase
  • Canvas 2D
Bulwark
Tower defence

Six towers, four tiers each, and a hundred generated approaches to your wall. What you researched last run decides what you can afford to build this one.

  • 100 approaches
  • 6 towers
  • Research tree
Chainbreaker
Deckbuilding roguelike

The Chain does not reset between turns. What you built last turn is still holding — and still costing you.

  • Deckbuilder
  • Persistent chain
  • DOM UI
Chorus
Puzzle platformer

Each breath lasts twenty seconds and leaves a self behind, frozen, holding an organ key. Your old selves are the platforms you climb.

  • 10 levels
  • Solver-proved
  • Ghost platforms
Cinder
Falling-sand sandbox

86 materials and one rule: density decides what sinks. Explosives need a fuse, and sleeping chunks are why it stays fast.

  • 86 materials
  • Chunk sleeping
  • Sandbox
Deeper
Descent

The bag is the whole design. Every rope, lamp and stone you take down is one you have to carry back — and something out there freezes when you light it.

  • Inventory puzzle
  • Light + dark
  • Canvas 2D
Dustline
Positional deckbuilder

A deckbuilding roguelike on a stretch of dead rail, where where you stand matters as much as what you play.

  • Positional
  • Roguelike
  • Canvas 2D
Gearworks
Logic puzzle

Route belts, spin the dynamo, and wire the bay until the machine runs. Every bay is solver-verified, so a solution always exists.

  • 100 bays
  • 100 wires
  • Solver-checked
Glide
Sliding puzzle

You do not stop when you want to — you stop when something stops you. Momentum is the whole problem, alone or against up to six others.

  • 100 levels
  • 2–7 players
  • Solver-proved
Hivebound
3D colony defense

Rule the colony as its queen. Weather and wave modifiers pick at your defenses; silk and jelly buy them back.

  • 3D / three.js
  • Waves
  • Jelly shop
Land / Fly
Arcade flight

Space takes off and space puts you down again. The hard part is always the second one.

  • Arcade
  • Landing
  • Canvas 2D
Lantern Hollow
Cozy tea garden

Six herbs pair into twenty-one teas. Guests never order by name — they tell you a mood, and you work out the cup.

  • 21 teas
  • Journal
  • Cozy
The Lighthouse Keeper
One-night sim

One beam pulled three ways: the ships that need it, the followers who gather in it, and the drowned keeper who wants it off.

  • One beam
  • One night
  • Canvas 2D
Market Day
Minigame gauntlet

Five stalls, five ingredients, five different games. You do not buy a burger here — you beat each stall for the part of it you want.

  • 5 minigames
  • 1 burger
  • Canvas 2D
MMMM
Precision platformer

A game about the letter M. Hold M to climb, release to fall, and the path you take draws the Ms behind you.

  • 100 levels
  • One button
  • Your signature
Mossford
Cozy 3D town

A small town that wants nothing from you but the post. Carry letters between the people who live here and the town fills itself in around the round.

  • 3D / three.js
  • Letters
  • No fail state
Nectar
Day / night sim

Forage by day, defend the hive by night. Raiders, weather and castes are all table-driven, so no two nights ask you for the same thing.

  • Day / night
  • Bees
  • Canvas 2D
Nine Lives
Fire simulation

Nine deaths, and each one permanently scars the room it happened in. You will run out of rooms before you run out of lives.

  • 9 lives
  • Fire sim
  • Permanent scars
Prototype
Game about games

Pick four modules, paint the level, then actually play the game you just built — and then sit there and get reviewed for it.

  • Level editor
  • Playtest
  • Reviews
Ring
Arcade

Nine concentric rings, one collapsing reactor, and a shrinking amount of time to get from the core to the outside.

  • 100 levels
  • 9 rings
  • Canvas 2D
Rustbucket
Metroidvania

Five sockets, and the parts you bolt into them are the whole progression. Every gate is just a part you have not dug out of the scrapyard yet.

  • 5 sockets
  • Swappable parts
  • Scrapyard
Sixty
Stealth

One perfect minute. The guards are deterministic, so the clock is the level — and you only learn the building by losing it.

  • 60 seconds
  • Deterministic
  • Learn by dying
Spellbound
Spelling bee

807 words across ten bands and a thousand seeded levels. Homophones arrive with their definition, because otherwise they would be unfair.

  • 807 words
  • 1000 levels
  • 10 bands
The Trade
Market game

Only you can see what a thing is really worth. Every trade you win makes someone suspicious, and suspicion is the other half of the scoreboard.

  • Market sim
  • Suspicion
  • Canvas 2D
The Understudy
Stage simulation

You can only work a puppet you are standing beside. The show goes on whether or not you got there in time.

  • Puppet show
  • Cues + ink
  • Tragedy
Undergrowth
2D sandbox

Dig, build, and light the dark. Ore tiers gate how deep you can go, torches actually cast light, and the boss only comes out at night.

  • Tile world
  • Real lighting
  • Night boss

Horror

the dark ones · dread, body horror, sound · play these with the lights however you like

Candle Fire
3D yard defence

Keep him out of the yard. Boards, traps and fire all buy you a night each, but blue light is the only thing that actually stops him.

  • 3D / three.js
  • Traps
  • Blue light
Halden Deep
First-person horror

Seven sublevels down, and everything living in them has a name in the logs before it has a shape in your torchlight. Twenty-seven datapads explain exactly how this happened.

  • 7 sublevels
  • 27 datapads
  • Raycast 3D
Last Call
3D bar sim

You inherited your dad's bar. Night one is pouring drinks and making rent; by night six it is neither of those things.

  • 6 nights
  • 3D / three.js
  • Tips + rent
Porridge
Fairy-tale horror

Goldilocks shot the bear. You are the bear. Forage while you are alive and haunt the house once you are not — three bowls, three endings.

  • Two acts
  • 3 endings
  • Canvas 2D
Rime
Sliding-puzzle horror

Halden's corridors with Glide's rule: once you push off, you do not stop. Every deck is solver-proved and every par was ground down by hand.

  • Solver-proved
  • Raycast 3D
  • Par times
The Rowan Line
Audio horror

You are the 911 dispatcher and the only thing you have is the line. There are no graphics. Choosing to say nothing is a move, and it is scored like one.

  • No graphics
  • Dispatch
  • Silence scored
Tallow
Top-down horror

Oil burns down whether or not you are using it. The Hollow is patient, and the pit at the bottom of Marrow Hill reaches back.

  • Lantern oil
  • Light + shadow
  • The Hollow
Unknown
Survey horror

Take a sounder down the Kettle Deep, drop beacons, and come back with the record. Nothing attacked the second team — they wanted that in the record.

  • Beacons
  • Dread meter
  • Strata
Vigil
Narrative horror

The knight killed the dragon. The dragon was the princess. Six days of the same grief in the same rooms, and only noticing what is wrong gets you out of it.

  • 6 days
  • Role swap
  • True ending

Tools & Toys

not games · things to make things with, and one thing to just watch

Inkflow
Paint & animation studio

Smooth brushes rather than pixels, with layers, frames and onion skinning. Strokes land on a scratch buffer first, so nothing commits until you lift the pen.

  • Smooth brushes
  • Layers
  • Onion skin
Maker
Game maker

Paint a level, then wire up WHEN/THEN blocks until it behaves. The rules are the engine — even the monsters are authored, not built in.

  • Rule blocks
  • Level painter
  • Play it
Pixel Forge
Pixel animation studio

Draw sprites frame by frame with layers and onion skin, then export the animation as a GIF the page encodes itself.

  • Layers + frames
  • Onion skin
  • GIF export
Tidepool
Ecosystem toy

No score and no way to lose. Stock the tank, then leave it alone and see whether what you put in there can keep itself going.

  • No score
  • No lose state
  • Watch only
