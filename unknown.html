A SOUNDING OF THE KETTLE DEEP

BRIEFING

There is no light in the Kettle Deep. Not dim light. None. Lamps were tried and lamps were lost, and what came back up the shaft afterward was not worth writing down.

So you go down with a sounder instead. You clap the dark and you listen to what comes back, and for about a second and a half you know where you are.

Then you don't.

Nine strata. Three beacons on each — set them all and the way down opens. A lit beacon holds a piece of the Deep in the known, permanently. That is the whole job: turn unknown into known, one small circle at a time.

It will cost you. A sounding is a shout. Everything down here has been waiting a long time for a shout.

"Nothing attacked the second team. I want that in the record. Nothing attacked them. They simply could not stand not knowing, and they ran, and the Deep is not a place you can run in." — Survey Master Ilke Rannoch, final log
