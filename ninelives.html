NINE LIVES

The house is burning. Your kittens are inside it. So are you.

You have nine lives and no more. Each one you spend is spent here — and the house keeps what it takes. Where you burn, the rooms turn to cinder. Where you drown, the water stays. Where the ceiling finds you, the rubble never lifts.

You come back changed too. That is the bargain.
