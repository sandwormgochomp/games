BLOBS

A very small 3D garden that runs on very little power.

Click the ground to spawn a blob. They wander off, eat sprouts, make goo, and split when they're full. Spend the goo on more blobs.

  • Ground blobs roam, eat and fight. Floaters hang in the air, drift about, and nothing down there can reach them.
  • Grab and fling any blob by dragging it — throw one over the wall and it's gone.
  • Right-drag to spin the camera, wheel to zoom.
  • 1–5 pick a species, Esc pauses.

Running at 30 fps on the low-power GPU. Change that any time with ⚙ POWER.
