Opacity vs Flow. Flow is how much paint each dab lays down — low flow builds up as you scrub back and forth. Opacity caps the whole stroke, so a 40% stroke stays 40% no matter how many times you cross it.

Hold makes one drawing sit on screen for several playback frames, so you can time an animation without redrawing it.

Onion skin ghosts the neighbouring frames of the layer you are drawing on — red behind, green ahead — and slips them in underneath your live artwork. That way a painted background never hides them. Keep your animation on its own layer and your backdrop on another.
