BRIEFING

  
Halden Deep was sealed after the VITRIS-9 incident. Sublevel 8 — the cryogenic wing — was the last section to lose power, and when the coolant loop ruptured it did not burn. It froze. Eleven metres of standing meltwater flash-froze across every deck plate in the wing.

  
The deck is black ice. You cannot walk on it. You push off, and you keep going until something stops you: a bulkhead, a patch of grit, a drum, the far wall. You do not choose where you stop. You only choose which way you leave.

  
Everything that lives down here is blind. There is no light budget for eyes eleven levels under. They hunt by sound, and on ice every single thing you do is a sound — the push, the run of your boots over plate seams, and worst of all the stop.

  
A slide that ends on grit is a whisper. A slide that ends against a bulkhead at full speed is a drumbeat through the entire wing.

  
Recover what you can from the wing. There is a lift at the far end of every deck. Twenty-four maintenance logs survived the freeze; how much of Halden you leave understanding is up to you.
