THE RULES OF MARROW HILL

Oil is time. The lantern drains whenever it is lit. Canisters are scattered through the wards.

Noise travels. Running is heard three wards away. Slamming a door is heard everywhere. The Hollow walks toward sound.

Sanity rots in the dark and near the Hollow. Red emergency lamps hold it back. Sedatives help. At zero, it always knows where you are.

Doors can be shut behind you. It takes the Hollow time to break one — and breaking one is very loud, which tells you exactly where it is.

Lockers hide you completely — but you are still breathing, and it will come and stand at the vent. Hold SPACE. You have about seven seconds of air. It usually takes less than that to lose interest. Usually.

Tallow is why you are still alive. Hold E over a body with the lantern lit and you can render it down — it takes nearly four seconds of kneeling with your back to the room, it burns oil, it is wet and loud, and it is worth it. Each body gives two or three candles.

A candle is a weak light and a very good witness. Set one with F and it burns for four minutes. The Hollow snuffs any flame it walks past. Check the map: gold means still burning, grey means something has been through there. Learn to read it before your nerve goes, because after that the flames start lying to you.

Total darkness is not empty. Stay in it for more than about twenty seconds and you will not come out of it. Any light resets that — the lantern, a flare, an emergency lamp.

The thing that climbs out cannot be hurt, cannot be outrun forever, and cannot be hidden from. It can only be looked at. While it is in your light it does not move at all. The moment it isn't, it starts again — faster the longer you leave it. Back away with it in your beam, and try not to think about what is behind you.

Your lantern is old and your nerve is not what it was. Neither the flame nor the readouts on this screen are entirely reliable.

The pit is not on any floor plan. There is a ledge around it. Walk the ledge, keep the wall on your left, and do not stop. Things come up out of it, and they are longer than you think. Light does not reach the bottom, so light will not tell you when one is coming.

Restoring power lights the whole building. It also wakes the Hollow permanently. Do not restore power until you know the way to the stairwell.
