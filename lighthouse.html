THE LIGHTHOUSE KEEPER'S

LAST NIGHT

Six hours until dawn, twenty-six hulls still out on the water, and the rocks of Carrick Sound waiting under every one of them.

Hold a ship in the beam and it will find the channel. Let it go dark and it will not.

Your father kept this light for thirty years. On his last night he wrote one line in the log: it comes up the stairs when the lamp is cold.

MOUSE  aim the beam
A / D  or  ← →  turn the lamp
SHIFT  focus the lens — narrow, burning, thirsty
SPACE  close the shutter — saves oil, hides you, blinds them
M  sound on / off

Smacks are quick and take a course almost at once. Coasters are steady. A packet boat is slow, sluggish on the helm, carries people, and counts double against you if you lose her.
