Dusk — the part that matters
Fifty dollars a night — that is the whole budget. Anything you do not spend is still there tomorrow, so saving two nights for a bear trap is a real plan.
Board a gap — a hole in the fence is an open door. $18 well spent.
Latch, then bolt — a latch buys seconds, a bolt buys a minute.
Move the bins — anything you can carry, left within 3m of the fence, is a step ladder. Carry it to the middle of the yard.
Repair panels — he pries boards loose. Damaged panels let him squeeze.
Motion lights — cheap, and they rout him. Bulbs burn out. He unscrews them.
Gravel — won't stop him, but you'll hear where he is.
Chimes — wake you the moment he touches that stretch of fence.
Thorn hedge — that panel can never be climbed again.
Night
Torch beam on him — he breaks off and runs. Costs battery, and boldness rises.
Shouting — instant rout at close range. Boldness rises a lot.
Bold enough and lights stop working on him. Then it's only the fence.
Nerve drains while he's close and you can see him. At zero you come to on the porch and he's had a free hour.
Don't crowd him. Watch him too long from too close and he stops being patient.
