BRIEFING

You are a spark trapped at the centre of a failing reactor. Nine concentric rings stand between you and the rim, and the core is expanding outward behind you. Ride the lanes, find the gaps, get out.

MOUSE  the spark chases your cursor around the lane
LEFT CLICK  dash outward — only passes through a gap
RIGHT CLICK  fall back one lane to dodge

← →  orbit by keyboard instead — either scheme works,
 whichever you touched last takes over
SPACE / ↑  dash outward  ·  ↓  fall back
R  restart  ·  ESC  pause

Gold shards are keys. Amber rings are sealed until you hold enough. Shutter rings breathe open and shut. Stay ahead of the front — the further ahead you finish, the more stars you keep.
