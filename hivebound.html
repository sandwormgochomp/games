THE QUEEN'S DUTIES

FLIGHT

  • W A S D — fly (relative to view)
  • Space — climb  ·  Shift / C — descend
  • Mouse — look. Click once to capture the cursor.

THE NINE CASTES

  • Worker — forages nectar; also pitches in on new comb
  • Guard — intercepts anything near the hive
  • Nurse — purges toxic gas, scrubs parasite clutches, mends eggs
  • Drone — escorts you and fires venom bolts
  • Mason — raises commissioned comb twice as fast as anyone, patches damage
  • Scout — ranges far out and marks enemies: everything hurts them +30%
  • Lancer — heavy shock trooper; its lance shatters armour for 4s
  • Attendant — shadows you, healing you and clearing gas around you
  • Silk Cutter — shears webs off the comb and does double damage to spiders

COMBAT

  • LMB — venom sting (mild aim assist)
  • Q or RMB — Wing Gust: blasts everything back and thins toxic gas
  • R — Royal Pheromone: heals and enrages the whole colony

ROYAL JELLY

  • Elites and warlords drop royal jelly. One jelly rerolls the boons offered after a wave.
  • T — The Queen's Chambers. Unlike boons, this is a fixed board you can open at any moment and buy from deliberately: nursery, vault, foundry, distillery, barracks, shear rack and weather eye, each dug deeper with jelly and nectar. The meadow does not stop while you shop.

THE COMB

  • 1–9 — select brood type
  • E — lay an egg into the nearest free cell (fly low over the comb)
  • Eggs hatch on a timer. Eggs are the colony's weak point.
  • B — commission a new ring of comb. Pale ghost cells appear and your nurses and workers fly out and build them, cell by cell. Every finished cell is one more egg you can raise. Hover over a ghost cell yourself to add your own wax.
  • G — raise a Wax Sentry in the nearest free cell. It shoots on its own and never sleeps, but that cell will never hold brood again — and free cells are the only thing limiting your colony. Gas rots them, grubs chew them, and anything attacking the comb goes for them first. Masons re-pack them.
  • Enter — call the next wave early during FORAGE

THE MEADOW

  • A Queen's Bloom opens out in the meadow every few forages — one flower holding six times what an ordinary one does, deliberately far from home. It is ringed on your radar. It does not refill: drain it and it closes. Going for it means leaving the comb.
  • Crab spiders are already out there. One sits inside a flower, motionless, casting no shadow, and does not show up as a threat until something comes within reach — then it breaks cover and seizes the forager, which stops flying and working until the spider is dead. They never come to the hive, so a wave can end with one still waiting.

THREATS

  • Wasps & Scouts — dive the comb and your bees
  • Dragonflies — hover above the comb and drop toxic gas onto your eggs. Nurses purge it; a Wing Gust thins it.
  • Chitin Beetles — armoured, crawl in and chew the stump
  • Armoured Ladybugs — that domed shell shrugs off two thirds of all damage. They land on the comb and glue clutches of parasite eggs onto your cells; a clutch left alone for 20 seconds hatches grubs that crawl the wax eating your brood. Hit one with a Wing Gust to flip it onto its back — helpless, and its armour counts for nothing. Nurses scrub clutches off, stings burn them, Lancers shatter shells.
  • Soldier Ants — never alone. A column marches overland for the stump, and any bee flying low gets pulled down on the way.
  • Wax Moths — flutter over the comb and drop live larvae straight onto the wax. No clutch to burn off — the grub is already crawling.
  • Robber Bees — fast, and they aren't here to fight. One dips into your stores, fills its crop and runs for the treeline. Let it cross and the nectar is gone. Shoot it down and the haul spills back.
  • Assassin Bugs — snipe from standoff range and refuse to close, so guards have to chase. Kill them yourself.
  • Meadow Widows — hang over the comb and spin silk across your cells. A webbed cell stops dead: the egg inside stops developing and slowly strangles, and you can't lay there. Silk Cutters shear it fastest; nurses pick at it; your stings and gusts tear at it.
  • Hornet Brutes — every 5th wave  ·  Warlords — every 10th, rotating through Mantis, Hornet Queen, Silk Broodmother and Legion Matriarch

SKY & TIDE

  • The weather turns over every phase and it is never just scenery. Rain washes toxic gas off the comb but weighs your wings down; a gale shoves everything with wings sideways; dry heat leaves the blooms slow to refill; nightfall makes them bolder.
  • From wave 4 most assaults also carry a modifier — shown under the phase name. SWARM, HARD SHELLS, FRENZY, FAMINE, SIEGE, BROODING, WINDBORNE. Read it before you commit your brood.
